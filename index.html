YT Scheduler

YT Scheduler is an Android creator productivity application that helps YouTube creators plan, format, schedule, and publish YouTube Shorts and long-form videos using deterministic publishing intervals.

Key Features of YT Scheduler

  • Deterministic Video Queue & Scheduling: Automatically spaces upcoming video uploads across customizable hourly intervals (0–24h) or Cron schedules using native YouTube publishAt scheduling.
  • Shorts Format Validation & Media Processing: Inspects local video resolution, orientation, and duration on-device, offering 9:16 vertical center-cropping, trimming, and visual filters prior to upload.
  • Resumable YouTube Uploads: Uploads creator-selected video files directly to the creator's authorized YouTube channel with chunk-level progress tracking and Wi-Fi-only network controls.
  • Channel Metadata & SEO Management: Allows creators to review their existing channel videos, generate title/description/tag suggestions, and update video metadata after explicit user approval.

How YT Scheduler Uses Google & YouTube APIs

YT Scheduler connects to the official YouTube Data API v3 and YouTube Analytics API only after you explicitly sign in with your Google Account via OAuth 2.0:

  • YouTube Upload (youtube.upload): Used strictly to upload and schedule local video files that you explicitly add to your publishing queue.
  • YouTube Channel & Metadata Management (youtube / youtube.readonly): Used to list your authorized YouTube channels, playlists, and videos so you can inspect and update titles, descriptions, tags, and scheduled publish times.
  • YouTube Analytics Read-Only (yt-analytics.readonly): Used to display your channel's own view, watch-time, and subscriber performance inside the app's dashboard.
