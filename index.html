YT Scheduler

Package: com.aistudio.ytscheduler.vxpq | Developer: muletrains2024@gmail.com

1. About the Application

YT Scheduler is an Android video queue and publishing automation application designed for YouTube creators to schedule video uploads at spaced intervals, analyze channel performance, and optimize titles, descriptions, and tags.

2. Purpose of the Application

YT Scheduler connects to YouTube API Services to:

  • View owned YouTube channel details and uploaded video roster (youtube.readonly).
  • Retrieve performance analytics including average view duration and watch time (yt-analytics.readonly).
  • Apply user-approved metadata optimizations (titles, descriptions, tags) using videos.update (youtube.force-ssl).
  • Upload scheduled video clips and 9:16 Shorts at creator-defined intervals (youtube.upload).

4. Privacy Policy & Data Protection

YT Scheduler stores OAuth access tokens securely on your local Android device using sandboxed private storage. We do not harvest, store, or sell user credentials or personal data to third parties. Permissions can be revoked at any time via Google Security Settings.
