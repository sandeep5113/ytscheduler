Privacy Policy — YT Scheduler

Effective Date: October 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how YT Scheduler ("we", "our", or "the Application") accesses, uses, stores, and protects user information when you use the YT Scheduler Android application and website (https://sandeep5113.github.io/ytscheduler/).

1. Information We Access and Collect

When you sign in to YT Scheduler using Google Sign-In / OAuth 2.0, we request access only to the scopes required to operate your scheduling studio:

  • Google Account Basic Profile & Email: Your email address and display name to identify your authenticated session and isolate your channel settings.
  • YouTube Data API v3 (youtube.readonly, youtube, youtube.upload): Used to display your YouTube channels, playlists, and uploaded videos; upload local video files that you place in your queue; and update video titles, descriptions, tags, categories, and scheduled publishAt timestamps when you approve changes.
  • YouTube Analytics API (yt-analytics.readonly): Used to retrieve read-only channel metrics (views, watch time, subscriber counts) for display on your personal dashboard.
  • Local Media Files: Video files that you explicitly select via the Android system media picker for local trimming, cropping, and uploading to YouTube.

2. Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use Disclosure)

YT Scheduler's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your Google or YouTube data to any third parties.
  • We do not use your Google or YouTube data for advertising, ad targeting, or creditworthiness evaluation.
  • All YouTube uploads and metadata updates occur strictly at your explicit direction.

3. Data Storage, Security & Retention

  • On-Device Encryption: OAuth 2.0 access tokens and API credentials are encrypted on your Android device using Android Keystore (`EncryptedSharedPreferences`).
  • Cloud Sync: Your queue metadata and creator profile preferences are stored in Google Cloud Firestore under strict owner-only security rules bound to your authenticated Firebase User UID.
  • Data Deletion & Revocation: You can clear your local queue and sign out inside the app at any time. You can also immediately revoke YT Scheduler's access to your Google Account at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

4. YouTube API Services Compliance

YT Scheduler uses YouTube API Services. By using YT Scheduler, you also agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and acknowledge the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Terms of Service

By using YT Scheduler, you confirm that you own or have all necessary rights and permissions for the video content and metadata you schedule and upload to YouTube. You are solely responsible for complying with the YouTube Terms of Service and YouTube Community Guidelines. YT Scheduler is provided "as is" without warranties of any kind.
